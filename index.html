Takeaway 1 · The Story

What Console Is

Persona-driven dashboard reporting, delivered pre-built
EMPYREAN SOLUTIONSINTERNAL USE ONLY

The product

Standardized data analytics and dashboarding designed specifically for financial institutions. Console turns raw financial data into persona-based dashboards, so leaders and product managers get answers without relying on Finance to source and distribute reports.

  • Sold as a separate license fee — an add-on available to any Empyrean customer, licensed as an asset-tiered annual license.
  • Native to Empyrean Dataverse — the same data foundation behind ALM, CECL and budgeting & planning.
  • Dashboards are not customizable at launch — but they are configurable to your own product, region and GL hierarchies.
  • No report authoring required — no SQL, no BI team, and minimal IT involvement at most institutions.
Access is role-based and admin-managed, scoped to institution, region, branch or department, with single sign-on at launch. Provisioning is owned by Finance, not IT.

What it does not do at launch

This is the canonical list — the whole deck points back here. Being precise protects the deal. Every one of these is a live roadmap conversation, not a gap we hide.

Scope guardrail Console reports on inputs only — imported data and actuals. Product outputs (ALM model results, profitability results, CECL calculations and planning outputs) are not surfaced at launch. Do not position or demo it that way.
  • No profitability metrics — no outputs from the profitability calculation engine.
  • No export or scheduled delivery — no PDF, no Excel, no email. Viewing means logging in.
  • No user-authored dashboards — configurable to the institution's hierarchies, but not customizable.
Two more limits worth knowing but rarely raised unprompted: data is prior-day, not real-time, and there is no mobile app. Everything else lives in the Internal FAQ.

Three page types

Every persona works the same path — land on the role view, filter to the segment that moved, then open the individual record behind it.

  • Homepage — the persona's landing dashboard: portfolio balances, volume trends and activity summaries for their role.

What It Takes To Go Live

Two steps, and the language to use about both
EMPYREAN SOLUTIONSINTERNAL USE ONLY

The path to value

  1. At activation Console activates with the license. Customers already on Dataverse see baseline metrics right away — instrument balances, yields, cost of funds, maturities and new production. Real, but not where the value sits.
  2. Data sourcing — timing varies Dashboards draw on data that may not be in Dataverse yet: FICO score, days past due, call codes, LTV, NAICS and risk ratings. Duration depends on which dashboards they want and how organized their data is.
  3. Implementation — 4 to 6 weeks Hierarchies and dimensions are configured and data is brought in to populate the dashboards. Essentials typically runs about 4 weeks, Plus closer to 6.
Net-new customers add a data foundation stage: daily instrument and GL imports, auto-upload via Azure DevOps, region and product hierarchies, GL and credit risk dimensions, employee codes and Customer 360, then security, roles and report pages.

Cost & sales guidance

One-time implementation fee
$18K – $26K for Essentials and $22.5K – $32.5K for Plus in the target asset bands. Billed separately from the annual license, alongside cloud hosting.
Three rules — do not break these
  • Do not say "immediate access on day one." Baseline metrics at activation are real, but leading with them sets up a disappointment later.
  • Treat 4 to 6 weeks as a planning range, not a commitment. Every institution differs and we will know more as implementations complete beyond beta.

Objections

The launch limits themselves are on slide 4 — this is how you say them under pressure. Acknowledge the concern, counter with evidence, then expand with a question that moves the conversation forward
EMPYREAN SOLUTIONSINTERNAL USE ONLY

"We are considering KlariVis given its strong brand in community banking."

Acknowledge
KlariVis has strong adoption and delivers good out-of-the-box analytics.
Counter
What is unique about Console is that it runs on the Empyrean Dataverse foundation you already have — a single source of data truth.
Expand
Who will build and maintain the KlariVis data feeds once they are live, and what happens when one breaks or a new source is needed?

"We use Power BI and our IT team supports it."

Acknowledge
Power BI is capable, and an IT-supported BI environment is a real asset.
Counter
Console is not a replacement for Power BI — it works alongside it. You already have the data; this surfaces it as metrics, personas and views that Power BI cannot deliver without a build.
Expand
How long does it take from when Finance requests a new report to when IT delivers it today?

"Analytics is not our priority right now."

Acknowledge
We hear that often, and every institution has competing priorities.
Counter
The institutions that say analytics isn't a priority are usually spending the most time on manual reporting without realizing it. The value is most obvious to the analyst losing hours a week — not just the executive seeing the output.
Expand
How much of your analyst's week goes to report production versus actual analysis, and is that the best use of their time?

"We're waiting for reference clients before investing."

Acknowledge
Wanting proof points before committing is completely reasonable.
Counter
We can walk you through a demo and share what we're hearing from early evaluations. Because your data already lives in Dataverse, you avoid the separate warehouse build other tools require.
Expand
What would make you comfortable moving sooner? Is there a specific dashboard you want the demo to focus on?

"Will it show our ALM and CECL results?"

Acknowledge
Great question, and it's important we're precise about scope.
Counter
Console reports on inputs — imported data and actuals from the same Dataverse foundation that feeds ALM, CECL and planning. One data foundation, no reconciliation between systems. Deeper cross-product insight is the direction the platform is heading.
Expand
Which matters more right now: analytics on your actuals across loans, deposits and GL, or a view of model outputs?

The four questions you will actually be asked

  • "Can we export it?" No at launch. No PDF, Excel or scheduled email — viewing means logging in. Say it early rather than at contract stage. Export is a December roadmap item.
  • "How current is the data?" Prior day. A file-based daily import, so dashboards are only as current as their last import.
  • "Can we change the dashboards?" Configurable, not custom. Their product, region and GL hierarchies shape every page, but nobody authors new views.
  • "Is our customer data exposed?" A limited subset. Name, ZIP, internal IDs and RM names. Restricted data is contractually refused.

Mobile, SSO, AI and the MIQ distinction come up less often and are answered in full in the Internal FAQ. Scope questions — model outputs, profitability — are the objection to the left.
